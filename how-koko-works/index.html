How Koko Pay Works in Sri Lanka

Koko is a buy now, pay later service in Sri Lanka. It lets you split a purchase into 3 equal instalments over 60 days at 0% interest, online and in participating stores.

This is an independent, unofficial guide based on Koko's website and terms, last checked on 7 November 2026. Terms can change, so check the Koko app or website for the current version.

How to pay with Koko

  1. Download the Koko app (App Store or Google Play).
  2. Online: choose Koko as your payment method at checkout.
  3. In-store: scan the merchant's QR code with the Koko app and tap to pay.
  4. Pay the first of 3 instalments at purchase, then the rest over 60 days.

For example, a Rs. 9,000 purchase would split into 3 payments of Rs. 3,000 at 0% interest.

Koko Pay FAQ

Does Koko charge interest or fees?

Koko advertises 0% interest and says there are "no admin fees, mark-ups or hidden charges". A fee can apply if you reschedule too often, as described below.

What happens if I can't pay on time?

Koko says each customer gets 2 free reschedules a year, and each one after that costs a flat Rs. 500, paid when you reschedule. A reschedule extends a payment by up to two weeks. You request it in the app, before the instalment's due date.

Can I use a debit card?

Yes. Koko says you can use a debit or credit card.

How much can I spend?

Koko evaluates each purchase rather than publishing a fixed limit. It says new customers have tighter limits, and that a longer history of successful repayments makes higher spending more likely.

How do refunds work?

Once the merchant starts a refund, Koko returns the money to the card you paid with. Koko says it takes 3 to 5 working days to reach your account.

Who can use Koko?

Koko's terms require you to be at least 18 years old. See the full terms for the other requirements.
